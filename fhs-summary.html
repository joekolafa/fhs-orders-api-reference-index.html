Issue 1 · Snowflake Share

Share is counting fundraising dollars as restaurant revenue

Firehouse's Round Up & Give donations and fundraising menu items (medallions, donation bottles) both appear in Share's "net sales" figure. Neither should — the dollars pass through to the charity partner, not the restaurant.

What's happening

Share's net sales number equals the sum of check.amount as returned by the Orders API. Per Toast's API reference, check.amount "includes discounts and service charges," which means Round Up & Give donations are baked into it. Separately, fundraising menu items (which Firehouse tags as deferred) are also included in Share's totals rather than excluded.

Together these two paths overstate Firehouse net sales by roughly $10,600 per day fleet-wide — or about 1.1% of reported revenue.

What to do about it

Recommendation
From Share's net sales query, subtract two things:
  1. Applied service charges where serviceChargeCategory = 'FUNDRAISING_CAMPAIGN'
  2. Selection prices where selection.deferred = true

Issue 2 · Your Orders API pulls

Discounts are not being pulled from the Orders API

The DISCOUNT_AMOUNT column in your reconciliation data shows $0.00 for all 308 stores on 4/17. Toast's data for the same day shows $59,481 in discounts.

What's happening

Discount dollars live on appliedDiscounts arrays in the Orders API response. These arrays appear at two levels of the order hierarchy — inside each check, and inside each selection within a check. Both levels need to be walked to get the full discount picture.

Based on the all-zero values across every store, your current pipeline either isn't reading these arrays or is dropping them during aggregation. That's roughly $59K/day in discount activity that isn't being captured.

What to do about it

Recommendation
When walking each order, sum discountAmount from both:
  1. check.appliedDiscounts[].discountAmount — the larger bucket at Firehouse (~82% of discount dollars)
  2. selection.appliedDiscounts[].discountAmount — item-level discounts, BOGOs, comps (~18%)

Issue 3 · Your Orders API pulls

Per-store net sales values swing in both directions

Your Orders API net sales values differ from Toast's data by different amounts and in different directions at different stores — suggesting multiple smaller issues rather than a single formula bug.

What's happening

At the fleet level your net sales total is ~$4,600 higher than Share, but that's the net of individual store discrepancies that range from −$689 at one store to +$776 at another. When the per-store errors don't move in a consistent direction, it usually points to aggregation logic that behaves differently based on order shape (e.g., includes certain orders that should be excluded, or sums from different fields depending on which payment types are present).

Two specific patterns worth investigating

Pacific-timezone stores show extra tickets. Store 10240 (Pajaro Dunes) shows 83 tickets in your data versus 81 in Keboola, Share, and Toast's direct data. This is the signature of a pipeline using a UTC time window instead of the businessDate filter — UTC midnight cuts through the local-evening hours on West Coast stores, which can pull in tail-end orders from the adjacent business day.

Some stores show net sales wildly disconnected from the actual data. Store 10240 reports $2,504.52 in your pipeline versus $1,728.95 actual — a $776 overstatement that isn't explained by tax, fundraising, or any single field. This is likely multiple issues compounding (possibly including test-mode orders, possibly summing from check.totalAmount instead of check.amount, or something else).

What to do about it

Recommendations
  1. Switch the Bulk Orders query from startDate/endDate to ?businessDate=YYYYMMDD — this eliminates timezone math entirely and gives each store its proper local-day window.
  2. Confirm these order-state filters are in place: order.createdInTestMode = false, order.deleted = false, order.voided = false, order.approvalStatus = 'APPROVED'. Test-mode alone affected 27 orders across 7 Firehouse stores on 4/17.
  3. If helpful, share the aggregation query or script with us and we'll help pin down the math differences store-by-store.

Suggested net sales formula

The simplest formula that reproduces Toast's net sales convention (matching Keboola/Toastweb to within rounding):

net_sales = Σ selection.price
where
    order.createdInTestMode = false
    and  order.deleted          = false
    and  order.voided           = false
    and  order.approvalStatus   = 'APPROVED'
    and  check.deleted          = false
    and  check.voided           = false
    and  selection.deleted      = false
    and  selection.voided       = false
    and  selection.deferred     = false   -- excludes gift cards AND fundraising items

-- If pipeline already uses check.amount as the base:
net_sales = check.amount
            − Σ appliedServiceCharge.chargeAmount
              where serviceChargeCategory = 'FUNDRAISING_CAMPAIGN'
            − Σ selection.price
              where selection.deferred = true

Applied to the 4/17 data, either form reconciles to Keboola's fleet total within 0.025% and to Share's within 0.05%. We have a longer reference doc with the field-by-field walkthrough if your data team wants the extended version.
